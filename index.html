Key Insights (First Nations)

  • Hospitalisations rose from 1,167 (2011) to 2,220 (2021), a 90.2% total increase over eleven years.
  • Young adults (17–25) account for 30.9% of First Nations hospitalisations vs 19.7% for Non-Indigenous Australians.
